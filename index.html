Lifestyle Advice for Snoring

  1. Sleep on your side — keep a pillow behind your back so you don't roll onto your back
  2. Stop smoking; avoid alcohol, especially within 3 hours of bedtime
  3. Finish dinner at least 3 hours before sleep; keep dinner your lightest meal
  4. Exercise 45 minutes daily
  5. Avoid sleeping pills or sedatives unless your doctor has reviewed your snoring

Why a Sleep Study?

  1. Tells simple snoring apart from obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) — sound alone cannot
  2. Measures severity: how often breathing pauses (AHI) and how low oxygen drops
  3. Untreated OSA raises the risk of high blood pressure, heart disease, stroke and diabetes
  4. Daytime sleepiness from OSA causes road and workplace accidents
  5. The result decides the right treatment — weight loss, device (CPAP/oral appliance), or surgery
  6. Gives a baseline to confirm treatment actually worked
